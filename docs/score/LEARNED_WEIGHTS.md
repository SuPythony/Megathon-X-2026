## Limitations

- Stage 1 targets are **modelled** (SAP/RdSAP), not measured; the weights say which levers drive the official modelled ratings.
- Stage 2 is ecological (cell averages); it does not prove the same relation inside a single home.
- Condensing vs non-condensing gas boilers cannot be told apart in recent EPC text (D-16), so heating variation within gas homes is understated.
- Renewables: few homes have PV, so its weight is the least stable (see the bootstrap range).
- Agreement with these targets is not proof of real-world savings; the physics twin remains the only source of energy and savings numbers.
