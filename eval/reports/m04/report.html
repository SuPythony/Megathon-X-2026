Served model: EBM plus a monotone tree correction

The served model keeps every EBM term and adds one named term, a LightGBM correction fitted to the EBM's residuals under the same physics constraints. Because LightGBM's constraints are not exact when its trees split on categories, the correction is replaced by its monotone envelope, which makes the constraint hold exactly. The waterfall still sums to the prediction. The sections below the comparison describe the EBM component; the Bayesian update, diagnostics and showcase outputs use the served hybrid.

What drives the prediction

EBM is additive: a home's prediction is the average home plus one contribution per feature (and per allowed pair), all in kWh/m²/yr. That makes every number auditable. We also compare EBM's importance ranking with TreeSHAP on LightGBM; when two different model families agree, the explanation is a property of the data rather than an artefact of one model.

Method and limits

    
Data, target and split

    
Why per-home R² cannot be high, and what we claim instead

      
NEED gives about ten coarse, banded fields per home. A large share of a home's energy use comes from the people living in it: thermostat setting, occupancy, hours at home, number of occupants. None of that is in the data, so no model of these inputs can explain it. The ceiling section estimates that limit directly.

      
What we claim: EBM is significantly better than simple regression (paired bootstrap), reaches most of the explainable variance, is very accurate at archetype level, and its 90% ranges are calibrated. In GreenTwin this model is only the starting point. Bayesian calibration (M09) then narrows each home's range using its own meter readings.

    

    
Choices that protect against leakage and overfitting
    
      
  • Split by property, stratified by type × age × floor band × region × heating fuel, assigned once with seed 42.
    • 
      
  • Hyperparameters chosen on tune; intervals calibrated on cal; test used only for the numbers on this page.
    • 
      
  • Council tax band excluded: GreenTwin's Property record does not carry it, so it would not be available at prediction time.
    • 
      
  • 20% of training homes have deprivation masked so the model learns an honest "unknown". The no-IMD test score is reported alongside.
    • 
      
  • Training on unique feature cells weighted by count gives the same least-squares fit as all rows with 22–27× fewer rows to fit.
